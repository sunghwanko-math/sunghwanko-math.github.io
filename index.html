I am a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Seoul National University, advised by Gye-Seon Lee.

My research focuses on Coxeter polytopes and reflection groups, particularly the associated convex real projective structures and their deformation spaces. More broadly, I am interested in Hilbert geometry, discrete subgroups of Lie groups, higher Teichmüller theory, and related questions in geometry and topology.
